Grimoire

            
A reader for the files of Cythera, the 1999 Macintosh game: its world, its
              people, its scripts and its rules, read straight out of the game’s own data.

            
Nothing is loaded yet. Opening it downloads the game’s installer from
              archive.org, about 28 MB, and keeps a copy in this browser so the next
              visit is instant. You can forget that copy at any time from the data-file menu.

            
On the map: pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to move,
              zoom onto a ring to go inside a cave or a town, and hold on anyone to
              see who they are.

Using this browser

  

    Drop the Cythera installer (.sit or Cythera.bin) anywhere
    on this page to open the whole game, or a Cythera Data file on its own — the
    .hqx as downloaded is fine, as is a MacBinary or AppleSingle copy. Whatever you
    open is remembered, so the next visit needs no network. Press ? for the keyboard
    shortcuts, / to search. The address bar tracks where you are, so any view can be
    bookmarked or sent to somebody.
